# Data and license notes

Scripture Study Ultimate keeps source and license information visible because Bible translations and study datasets have different rights.

- Berean Standard Bible display and concordance data: CC0 / public domain as published by BSB Publishing. The app uses the Strong's aligned display, cross references, concordance, and geography outputs when downloaded.
- BSB `index-cc-by` morphology data: CC BY 4.0. Required attribution: Hebrew morphology data from Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible (OSHB), licensed under CC BY 4.0.
- World English Bible: public domain.
- American Standard Version 1901: public domain.
- King James Version: public domain in many jurisdictions. Crown rights and special rules may apply in the United Kingdom.
- Ang Dating Biblia 1905: public domain edition used through an open Bible source.
- Reina Valera 1909, Louis Segond 1910, Luther 1912: historic public domain editions where sourced.
- STEP Bible datasets: many are published under CC BY 4.0. They are upstream references for Greek, Hebrew, lexicon, and morphology work.
- Open Bible geography datasets: attribution required for CC BY data when used.
- New Living Translation: copyright Tyndale House Foundation. Not bundled as a complete text.
- English Standard Version (ESV): copyright Crossway. Not bundled as a complete text. Crossway permission or an authorized integration is required for uses beyond its standard guidelines.
- Ang Bible: Pinoy Version: copyright Philippine Bible Society. Not bundled as a complete text.

Always verify the terms of a translation or dataset before commercial redistribution in your jurisdiction.
